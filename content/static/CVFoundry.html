Problem

Most professionals maintain career content as scattered documents. In regulated and client-sensitive environments, this becomes harder: you need multiple variants, consistent language, and privacy-safe versions you can share.

Solution

CV-Foundry treats career content like a product: structured canonicals plus templates and profiles that render consistent outputs. The result is fast iteration without breaking trust.

Canonical YAML + narratives
Profiles for targeting
Templates for formats
Outputs are generated

What it produces

  • Resume variants for different roles and audiences
  • One-pagers for executive quick reads
  • Career communications kits and reusable narrative assets

Privacy by default

  • Canonicals can store real client context
  • Outputs default to anonymized, safe-to-share labels
  • Audience selection controls public vs actual rendering

How it works

  • Canonicals in YAML define roles, wins, skills, education, training
  • Profiles pick what to emphasize and how to constrain length
  • Templates render consistent artifacts (Markdown, HTML today)
  • Build generates outputs; do not hand-edit outputs
